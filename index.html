What is AO3 saver?

ao3saver is a browser extension that helps you keep works you have previously viewed. Install the extension, and each time you visit a work my server will archive a copy of it for you!

What does AO3 saver do with it's downloaded works?

ao3saver mirrors downloaded works for readers to access, nothing more.
None of the data on ao3saver will ever be fed into an ai or used to train ai. I do what I can to actively block search engines, ai's and similar automations from accessing any data on ao3saver. As a user, you can help me curb potential crawlers by not posting direct links to works on ao3saver on social media. Posting links online is how such crawlers find their targets, keeping it to chatrooms or dms mitigates this. This is true of any link to any website!

I'm an author and I want my fic removed from ao3 saver. What can I do?

I'll respect all takedown requests from authors! Just email me at mail@ao3saver.com. I can either:

  • Show a "this work has been removed" page in place of your work
  • Show a "this work has been removed" page in place of your work, with a message of your choosing to readers who are seeking your work
  • Show a "not found" error in place of your work
  • Redact your author name from the work, but still keep it hosted
When you email me, let me know which option you would like and provide a list of the works you would like removed.

Please do consider that if people are seeking out your works, it's likely personally important to them. I won't index your works for others to search, and I do actively block search engines and ai from indexing/scraping ao3saver. The only people who are able to access your work from my server are people with a direct link to it.
